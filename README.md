## 🌱 Why WordGameTrainer Exists

Every word-game player knows the feeling. You stare at seven tiles, and your brain freezes. Then someone across the table calmly lays down a seven-letter bingo and the wind leaves your sails. That moment is not about luck. It's about preparation. WordGameTrainer is the preparation.

Think of it as a gymnasium for the letters of your language. Instead of dumbbells, you get anagrams. Instead of reps, you get recall drills. Instead of a spotter, you get a coach who remembers every letter blend you've ever fumbled and quietly pushes you back toward it until mastery sticks. The ambition here is not simply to help you memorize lists — it is to rewire the instinctive pathway between seeing three consonants and speaking a winning word.

This project was assembled with the belief that every word-game enthusiast — casual or tournament-bound — deserves a training environment that adapts to them rather than expecting them to adapt to it.

## 🧩 The Idea Behind the Name

The **mara-nel/WordGameTrainer** lineage inspired this project. WordGameTrainer is a deliberate evolution of that spirit: it retains the focus on vocabulary mastery, but extends it with a modular training engine, an adaptive difficulty curve, and a multilingual spine that lets you study more than one lexicon without leaving the same panel.

Where the earlier concept treated the game as a puzzle, this repository treats it as a discipline. A discipline needs a dojo. This is the dojo.

## ✨ Feature Highlights

- 🎯 **Adaptive Drill Modes** — the trainer watches which letter combinations slow you down and quietly increases their frequency in future rounds.
- 📖 **Curated Lexicon Packs** — hundreds of thousands of validated words organized by length, difficulty, and thematic usefulness.
- 🔤 **Anagram Sprint** — race the clock to rearrange letters into as many valid words as possible.
- 🧠 **Recall Rehearsal** — spaced repetition woven into word study so the short, ugly words (the two-letter powerhouses) stay sharp.
- 🧮 **Score Projection Sandbox** — pre-play a move and see the exact score impact before you commit.
- 🕹️ **Blitz Mode** — a rapid-fire pace for players who want to sharpen reactions.
- 🗺️ **Progress Atlas** — a visual map of your journey across letter groups, complete with heatmaps of your strengths and blind spots.
- 🔁 **Session Replay** — rewind any completed drill to watch where you hesitated.
- 🧾 **Exportable Study Journals** — carry your notes out in clean, human-readable formats.
- 🌙 **Dark & Light Themes** — study at midnight without melting your eyes.
- 🧩 **Custom Word Lists** — build a personal deck from a novel, a textbook, or a tournament cheat sheet you're allowed to use (check your tournament rules first).
- 🎁 **Offline Mode** — no network needed for the core trainer once your lexicon packs are cached.

The layout breathes. On a wide desktop it pours into columns, heatmaps, and side panels. On a tablet it collapses into a two-pane tactile canvas. On a phone it becomes a single-handed mosaic of cards you can swipe between.

## 🌍 Multilingual Word Support

Vocabulary is culture. A trainer that only speaks one language is a trainer that only serves one culture.

WordGameTrainer ships with lexicon modules for multiple languages, each reviewed against accepted tournament word sources for that language family. You can:

- Switch lexicon mid-session without losing your streak.
- Compare a word's validity across two tongues side-by-side.
- Load regional variants (for example, different national spellings) as separate packs.
- Sync your progress per language so your English rating and your Spanish rating are tracked independently.

The architecture is deliberately plugin-shaped. Adding a new language is a matter of dropping in a lexicon file and a small manifest. The community keeps this growing.

## ⏳ How It Fits Into Your Day

You do not need a three-hour block to benefit. Ten minutes at a bus stop, five minutes before a match, three minutes while your tea steeps — all of it compounds. The trainer was designed around the idea of micro-commitments that aggregate into macro-competence.

A typical week might look like:

- **Monday** — 5-minute anagram sprint to wake up the pattern-recognition muscle.
- **Tuesday** — Recall rehearsal on two-letter words.
- **Wednesday** — A single full-length scoring sandbox puzzle.
- **Thursday** — Multilingual swap: study the same root in a second language.
- **Friday** — Blitz mode, just for fun.
- **Saturday** — Long-form study journal review.
- **Sunday** — Rest. Your brain will thank you.

The trainer never scolds you for missing a day. It simply welcomes you back.

## 🚀 Getting Started Without a Manual

We dislike manuals. A trainer should teach you the way a good board-game host does — by inviting you to sit down.

1. Open the application.
2. Pick a lexicon pack. If you're unsure, let the trainer pick for you.
3. Choose a drill mode. If you're unsure, choose "Anagram Sprint."
4. Play for three rounds.
5. Glance at your Progress Atlas. That's your new baseline.
6. Repeat tomorrow.

That's the whole onboarding. Everything else you'll discover by tugging on the edges of the interface like a curious child. Nothing breaks.

## 🔄 The Learning Loop

The trainer follows a simple philosophy borrowed from athletic training: **load, adapt, rest, repeat.**

- **Load** — the drill presents words slightly above your current comfort zone.
- **Adapt** — the engine recalibrates difficulty after every round.
- **Rest** — spaced repetition schedules ensure you don't over-stress a single letter pattern.
- **Repeat** — consistency outperforms intensity, always.

Players who follow the loop for a month report a noticeably faster recall of obscure two- and three-letter words without ever feeling like they "studied."

## 🔥 Scoring Metaphor: The Forge

Think of your vocabulary as raw metal. Every session is a strike of the hammer. The forge doesn't care if you're in a good mood today — it just keeps the rhythm. Over weeks, the metal is no longer raw. It becomes a blade. On game day, you draw it, and it cuts cleanly.

The scoring sandbox exists to show you exactly how much metal each strike is worth. It doesn't tell you what to play. It tells you what a play would cost and return. The decision is yours, and that is the point.

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Stewardship

Your study data belongs to you. The trainer stores progress locally by default. If you enable synchronization, your data is transmitted over encrypted channels and never sold. No third-party trackers ride along. There is no telemetry that leaves your device unless you explicitly opt in.

If you choose to delete your account, everything associated with it is wiped — not archived, not anonymized for later use. Gone.
